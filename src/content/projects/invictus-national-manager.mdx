## Overview
**Invictus National Manager** is a football management simulator for leading any national team.

## Features
- **222 national teams:** start a new game and take charge of any of them.
- **Save slots:** three save slots to keep several careers going.
- **Mod editor:** edit players, countries, clubs and stadiums.
- **Settings:** theme, match speed and auto-save.
