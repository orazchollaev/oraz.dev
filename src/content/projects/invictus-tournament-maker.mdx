## Overview
**Invictus Tournament Maker** is a football tournament simulator. It runs in the browser with data stored locally, and as an Android app on [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/dev?id=5598086025481113545).

## Features
- **Four competition formats:** knockout brackets, group stage plus knockout, leagues with promotion, relegation and optional playoffs, and Swiss-style tournaments.
- **Results:** enter scores manually or simulate a single match, a matchday, or the whole tournament. Surprise factor, home advantage and form settings tune the randomness.
- **Live matches:** minute-by-minute play with events, extra time and penalty shootouts for knockout ties.
- **Manager mode:** control a team's tactics, lineup and substitutions during matches.
- **Team management:** create teams and coaches, generate players in bulk, and view match history and stats.
- **Multiple seasons:** run repeat seasons with random, seeded or manual draws, and browse all-time history and statistics.
- **Customization:** 15 languages, light, dark and World Cup themes, bracket styles, music, and JSON export and import.
